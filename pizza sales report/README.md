## OBJECTIVE:

        This project features a comprehensive Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard designed to provide actionable insights into sales trends, customer behavior, and    product performance. By analyzing a year's worth of data (2015), the dashboard identifies peak periods, popular pizza categories, and revenue drivers to help optimize inventory and marketing strategies.

## Dataset Overview and Data Preprocessing:
         The dataset includes sales data, customer profiles, and 
         store information. Preprocessing involves aggregating sales data, 
         calculating customer metrics, and creating geographical visualizations.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
    Total Revenue: 817.86K

    Total Orders: 21,000

    Total Pizzas Sold: 50,000

    Average Order Value: 38.31

    Average Pizzas Per Order: 2.32
## Executive Summary of Insights
1. Sales Trends
* Daily Trends: Weekend demand is highest, with Friday leading as the busiest day of the week (3.5K orders), followed closely by Thursday and Saturday.

* Monthly Trends: Maximum order volume occurs in July (1,935 orders) and January (1,845 orders), suggesting seasonal peaks during mid-summer and the start of the year.

2. Product Performance
* Category Dominance: The Classic category is the top performer, contributing to the maximum number of sales and total orders (14,888 pizzas sold).

* Size Preference: Large-sized pizzas are the primary revenue drivers, accounting for nearly 46% of total sales, followed by Medium (30%) and Small (22%).

3. Inventory & Operations
* The business maintains a consistent average of 2.32 pizzas per order, indicating that customers frequently order for groups or families.

* "Veggie" and "Chicken" categories show healthy competition, each capturing roughly 24-25% of the market share.
