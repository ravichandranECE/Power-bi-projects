## Car Sales Analysis Dashboard
    A comprehensive data visualization project designed to track and analyze car sales performance. This dashboard provides real-time insights into Year-To-Date (YTD) and Month-To-Date (MTD) sales metrics, helping stakeholders identify trends, top-performing brands, and customer preferences.


##  Project Overview
    This interactive dashboard (built with Power BI/Tableau) transforms raw sales data into actionable business intelligence. It features two main views:

    Overview: High-level KPIs and trend analysis.

    Detail: Granular, row-level transaction data for deep-dive auditing.

# Dashboard overview:

1. YTD sales weekly trend : Display line chart illustrating the weekly trend of ytd sales.

2.YTD total sales by body style : visualize the distribution of ytd total sales across different car body styles using a pie chart.

3.YTD total sales by color : Present the contribution of various car colors to the YTD total sales through a pie chart.

4.YTD total sales by dealer region : showcase the ytd sales data based on different dealer region using a map chart to visualize the sales distribution geographically.

5.comapny wise sales trend in grid form : provide a tabular grid that display the sales trend for each company.

6.Details grid showing all car sales information : create a detailed grid that present all relevant information for each car sales.


Key Features & Insights
1. Executive KPIs (YTD & MTD)
Total Sales: Tracks the $371.19M YTD revenue with a 23.59% growth indicator.

Average Price: Monitors pricing health, currently at $28.09K per vehicle.

Units Sold: Visualizes the volume of cars sold (13K YTD) with percentage growth vs. previous periods.

2. Interactive Sales Trends
Weekly Trend: A line-and-area chart identifying seasonal spikes (notably a massive peak around week 38-40).

Body Style & Color Distribution: Donut charts showing that Pale White is the dominant color preference and visualizing the market share of different body types.

Geospatial Analysis: A map view pinpointing sales density across the United States, including key regions like Austin, Janesville, and Scottsdale.

3. Company-Wise Performance
A detailed leaderboard ranking manufacturers (Cadillac, Saab, Lexus, etc.) by their average price and total sales contribution to the overall GT (Grand Total).

4. Granular Detail View
A dedicated "Detail" tab providing a searchable table of every transaction, including Car_id, Customer Name, Dealer Name, and specific Model details.
