🏥 Healthcare Provider Performance Dashboard

A comprehensive data analytics project designed to track and analyze healthcare operational efficiency and financial performance. This dashboard provides stakeholders with real-time insights into patient volume, billing trends, and departmental resource allocation.

📊 Project Overview

This multi-page interactive dashboard (built with Power BI) visualizes key performance indicators (KPIs) for healthcare providers. It enables deep-dive analysis of patient stays, billing accuracy, and departmental revenue to improve decision-making and patient care outcomes.


## Key Features & Insights
1. Financial Analytics
    Billing Overview: Monitors a total billing amount of £3M with detailed breakdowns for medication costs (£546K) and treatment costs (£3M).

    Revenue Streams: Analyzes insurance coverage (£2.23M) versus out-of-pocket expenses (£1.13M).

    Departmental Revenue: Identifies Cardiology (£846,925) and Orthopedics (£813,253) as the top-performing departments by billing volume.

    Procedure Costs: Tracks procedure-specific billing, with X-Rays (£1.05M) and CT Scans (£0.81M) generating the highest revenue.

2. Operational Metrics
    Patient Volume: Tracks a total of 5,000 visits, with 1,232 admissions and 3,768 outpatients.

    Length of Stay (LOS): Visualizes patient distribution across stay durations, showing that the majority of patients stay for 5-7 days (1.04M patients) or 2-4 days (1.03M patients).

    Emergency vs. Non-Emergency: Breaks down visits by department, with General Surgery showing the highest volume of non-emergency visits (823).

3. Temporal Trends
    Year-over-Year Analysis: Compares monthly billing performance between 2024 and 2025.

    Weekday Performance: Provides a granular view of daily billing across departments, highlighting peak activity on specific days like Wednesdays and Fridays for Cardiology and Orthopedics.

    Quarterly Insights: Tracks seasonal fluctuations, noting peak billing in Q-1 (£1.7M).
   
#  Dashboard Functionality
    Dynamic Filtering: Filter data by Year, Quarter, and patient Race.

    Geospatial Analysis: Interactive map view for tracking patient locations by city and state across the United Kingdom.

    Service Type Breakdown: Analyzes the mix of Emergency, Inpatient, and Outpatient services for various diagnoses like Hypertension and Asthma.

## Use Cases
    Financial Planning: Optimizing billing cycles based on procedure and medication cost trends.

    Resource Management: Allocating staff and bed space according to department-specific visit volumes and stay durations.

    Insurance Strategy: Understanding out-of-pocket trends to improve insurance billing processes.
