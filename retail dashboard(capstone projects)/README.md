
# PROJECT POWER BI
## DOMAIN
    RETAIL
Multi-Category Sales & Product Performance Dashboard
## Overview
    This project presents an end-to-end data visualization solution for analyzing global sales performance across three core categories: Clothing, Electronics, and Food. Spanning data from 2020 to 2022, the dashboard provides a deep dive into product trends, revenue scenarios, and customer demographics to drive data-informed business decisions.
     
### High-Level Global Metrics
    Total Revenue (Sum of Order Total): 5M

    Total Price: 507.2K

    Total Products Sold: 49.7K

    Average Sale Value: 5.1K
## Key Business Insights
1. Product Trend Analysis
* Category Performance: "Clothing" is the leading category with 1.75M in total orders, followed by "Food" (1.65M) and "Electronics" (1.58M).

* Daily Patterns: Sales activity peaks mid-week on Wednesday (77.9K) and reaches its weekly high on Friday (78.7K).

* Seasonal Trends: Significant growth is observed during the second quarter, peaking in June (0.51M orders).

2. Sale & Revenue Scenarios
* Revenue by Category: Clothing generates the highest revenue (182K), followed by Food (165K) and Electronics (160K).

* Payment Preferences: Credit cards are the most popular payment method (173K), slightly outperforming PayPal (168K) and Cash (166K).

* Yearly Stability: Total revenue remained relatively stable across 2020, 2021, and 2022, with each category maintaining a consistent share of the total.

3. Product & Customer Performance
* Top Markets by Revenue: China is the top-performing country (82K), followed by Indonesia (62K) and Russia (34K).

* Top Cities: San Jose and Itumbiara lead city-level revenue at 2.0K each.

* Product Leaders: "Muffin" leads in order volume (24K), while "Ham" products represent some of the highest individual sales totals.

* Customer Demographics: The customer base is nearly evenly split between Male (46%) and Female (44%) users.

## Technical Features
    Multi-Page Navigation: Separate views for Trend Analysis, Revenue Scenarios, and Performance metrics.

    Dynamic Filtering: Interactive slicers for Categories, Dates (2020–2022), and Order Status.

    Geospatial Mapping: Global distribution of orders by country and category.

    Process Tracking: Analysis progression indicators (Analysis 1, 2, 3, and Summary) for structured reporting.
